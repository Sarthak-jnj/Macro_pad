**Opentop macropad is designed to keep the main PCB visible to external eyes to admire the technology that goes into everyday things rather than just looking at closed shells.** 

**Parts:**

**MCU:** 1x Seeed Studio XIAO RP2040

**Switches:** 6x MX-Style Switches

**Keycaps:** 6x white blank DSA keycaps

**Encoder:** 1x EC11 Rotary encoder with integrated push-button

**Display:** 1x 0.91" SSD1306 I2C OLED (128x32)

**Lighting:** 6x WS2812B LEDs

**Other:** 6x through-hole 1N4148 Diodes, 4x M3x5mx4mm heatset inserts, 4x M3x10mm screws.
